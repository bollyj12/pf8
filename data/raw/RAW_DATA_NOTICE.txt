The execution environment used to build this ZIP could not reliably transfer the
other Zenodo raw files because Zenodo returned HTTP 429/rate-limit responses.
The downloader is included with official filenames and published MD5 hashes, so
running it locally retrieves the exact raw source files and verifies integrity.
